## Goal

A peer with three extras — always on, holds the whole log, its grant carries authority — that can publish permissions by sync, overrule with a reason instead of rejecting, and detect and heal divergence.

## Decisions needed

D10

## Tasks

- [ ] `setPolicy(engine, partition, doc)` — `_policy` row, deny-all, keyed by the instance it governs; every validator prefers the synced doc over the bundle
- [ ] `correct(engine, { event, table, key, reason, detail, author, partition }, fix)` — the overwrite and its reason in **one** signed event; `_corrections` deny-all; `mesh.corrections.{all, mine, forEvent}`
- [ ] Digests: `engine.digest()` / `rowDigests()` as a **sum** mod 2^64 (XOR self-cancels); `divergentTables / divergentRows / rowRecords / repairRows` — repair is the ordinary merge, adopts no cursors
- [ ] Digests on the last catch-up frame, carrying their interest so cross-scope mismatches do not false-alarm

## Watch out

- The authority cannot *reject*: an unreachable peer keeps the provisional value with nothing ever contradicting it. Rejection is an overwrite with a reason.
- An absent table and an empty one must digest identically.
- A mismatch in a mixed-version fleet is ambiguous (RFC-0013 G18).

## Tests

- `policy-sync.test`, `corrections.test` (a member's forged correction is denied at its author and never applies at a peer; fix + reason is one event), `digest.test` (order-free; duplicate row changes the digest; symmetric repair; no cursors adopted).

## Done when

Permission changes deploy by sync with no app release; two deliberately diverged peers heal on next catch-up.
