## Goal

A signed proof that a device belongs to an account with a role in specific partitions, verified offline by every peer against the issuer's public key. No users, sessions or membership in the engine.

## Decisions needed

D08

## Tasks

- [ ] `issueGrant(issuer, { account, device, role, partitions, issuedAt, expiresAt })` → wire bytes
- [ ] `verifyGrant(wire, issuerId, now)` → `Result<GrantCore, Malformed | BadSignature | Expired>`
- [ ] `GrantRegistry(issuerId, now)`: `register` (newest-issued wins; a stale replay cannot downgrade; expiry reads as absent), `coreFor`, `wireFor` (the same bytes), `allWires`, `onRegistered`, `revoke` (local for now)
- [ ] Grants travel **first** in every handshake (E11, E12 consume this)
- [ ] A worked login route in the docs: your session → `issueGrant` → client registers

## Watch out

- Decide the partition-id form in grants vs events (D07) — `["acme"]` vs `"org:acme"` cost a debugging cycle.
- `expiresAt` is a staleness bound, not revocation. Short validity + renewal is the containment; propagation is E21.

## Tests

- `grants.test`, `grant-exchange.test` (a brand-new member's events apply at strangers because the grant travelled ahead).

## Done when

A forged event from a hacked client passes signature and quarantines on policy at every other peer; a stale grant replay does not downgrade.
