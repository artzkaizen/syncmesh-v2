## Goal

The bulk pipe: same frame link, much wider, on-demand lifecycle.

## Decisions needed

D12

## Tasks

- [ ] Native module over Wi-Fi Aware / NAN
- [ ] `class WifiAware extends FrameTransport`
- [ ] Route scoring prefers it for bulk when open — and it is the **first transport that means
      `costly` and `dormant`** (E28, moved here because only this epic can close it). Both flags
      exist in the scorer and neither is declared by anything: BLE opts out of `costly` on
      purpose, since low energy is the whole of what BLE is, and the relay declares nothing. So
      `COSTLY_SMALL`, `COSTLY_SMALL_URGENT`, `DORMANT` and the presence refusal that depends on
      `dormant` are exercised only by the scorer's own tests until this ships. Declaring them
      here is what makes an expensive radio refuse a frame too small to have paid for its power,
      and what stops presence being the thing that wakes it.
- [ ] Declare `maxLinks` (1–2 per RFC-0012 §1) so the mesh's budget sweep applies to it — the
      enforcement is built (E28) and reads whatever a medium states.

## Watch out

- Lifecycle is on-demand; do not hold it open.

## Tests

- A bulk payload never selects BLE while a faster link is open; live-event latency is unaffected during a concurrent snapshot transfer.

## Done when

A snapshot join between two phones goes over Wi-Fi Aware while presence stays on BLE.
