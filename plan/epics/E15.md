## Goal

Bytes that do not belong in a log: content-addressed, verified on put and on fetch, travelling out of band.

## Decisions needed

D18

## Tasks

- [ ] `hashOf` (sha-256), `verifyBlob`, `MemoryBlobStore`; `BlobCorrupt / NotFound / Timeout` as values
- [ ] Relay channel (E12) and client cache; `mesh.blobs.put / fetch`
- [ ] Persistent local blob store over the SQLite driver
- [ ] Answer in the RFC first: who must hold a blob; delete vs offline reference. Then refcount GC and per-partition quotas

## Watch out

- Junk must not squat a hash — verify **before** storing.

## Tests

- `blobs.test` (zero-byte, duplicate put idempotent, corrupt rejected, timeout).

## Done when

A photo round-trips through the relay verified both ways; a raw radio reports `NoSuchCapability` rather than pretending.
