## Context

Two phones with no internet must decide whether each other's writes are admissible. A session token cannot do that — your server is not in the path.

## Options

- **A · Device Ed25519 key = peer id; a signed grant from an issuer key binds account + device + role + partitions + validity; verified offline by every peer against the issuer's public key.** No users, sessions or membership in the engine — your auth issues the grant after it has said yes.
- **B · JWT from your provider, verified offline against cached JWKS** (Jazz's model) — reuses your existing tokens; peers cache the public key; claims drive policy. Harder to bind to a device key.
- **C · Both** — a grant is what the engine checks; your server mints it *from* a JWT. That is A with B as the on-ramp.

## What the research points to

A (which is C in practice): the grant is the boundary, `account` in config is a convenience the validator never trusts. Short validity with renewal is the real containment for a stolen device — expiry reads as *absent*, not as revocation. Revocation propagation is its own epic (E21).
## Watch out

Grants must travel *first* on every connect or events from an unknown author quarantine on `NoGrant`. Do not let validation depend on identity being configured: schema checks must run in an ungranted mesh too.

## Decision

**A — the device's Ed25519 key is the peer id; a signed grant from the issuer key binds account, device, role, partitions, claims and validity; every peer verifies it offline against the issuer's public key.** C in practice: your server mints the grant from whatever auth it already trusts.

- The engine has no users, sessions or membership. The grant is the boundary; `account` anywhere else is a convenience the validator never trusts.
- Grants carry **claims** — free-form JSON the app's server puts in (member id, permission matrix, entity ids) — because in real apps roles are data, not a fixed ladder; `allow` rules read claims through `claim(name).has(column)` (E06).
- Expiry is a staleness bound, not revocation: an expired grant reads as *absent*. Short validity with renewal (hours for apps with dynamic access, days for personal apps) is the containment for a stolen device; propagation of revocation is E21.
- Grant vectors are frozen alongside the event vectors from a fixed test seed; a port must reproduce them byte-for-byte.
