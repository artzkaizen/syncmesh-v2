## Context

BLE is 3–50 KB/s with a 128 KiB reassembly cap; a photo in the event log blocks convergence for everything and is replicated to every peer forever.

## Options

- **A · Content-addressed side channel** — rows carry the sha-256, bytes travel outside the log; the relay verifies before storing (junk cannot squat a hash), the client verifies on fetch. Memory cache first; persistent store, range fetch, refcount GC and per-partition quotas later.
- **B · Blobs in the log** — simple, and exactly the failure mode above.

## What the research points to

A. The open questions with teeth are *who is obliged to hold a blob* and what happens when a delete races an offline reference — answer them in the RFC before building GC.
